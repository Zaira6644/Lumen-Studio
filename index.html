About Lumen

Started in a two-chair room, still run the same way.

Lumen opened in 2015 as a two-chair studio above a bakery. We've grown to six artists and a proper skin room, but the original rule hasn't changed: no more clients booked in a day than we can actually give our full attention to.

What we hold onto

01

Honest consults

If a look won't suit your hair or your week, we'll say so before we start, not after.

02

Unrushed chairs

We schedule real time between appointments so the last client of the day gets the same attention as the first.

03

Product honesty

We'll tell you which product actually matters and which one is optional — no ten-step upsell.
